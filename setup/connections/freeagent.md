# FreeAgent: not supported yet

Money View OS can't connect to FreeAgent yet. There is no Claude connection for FreeAgent, and Money View works only through a live connection to the accounts app. It does not read exports, spreadsheets or reports saved into a folder.

If the person uses FreeAgent, follow the "not supported yet" paragraph in `setup/money-01-your-accounts.md`: say it in one plain paragraph, leave step 2 unticked and stop. Don't set anything up, don't suggest a workaround, and don't ask them to export anything.
