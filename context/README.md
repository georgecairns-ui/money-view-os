# Context

Claude's notes about the business and its money. Claude fills these in during setup and keeps them up to date as it learns more, always asking first.

They're plain text. You can open and edit any of them yourself. If something's wrong, just tell Claude and it'll fix it.

| File | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `business.md` | What the business does, for whom, and what it sells |
| `money.md` | Your accounts software and how you invoice, your bank and tax pot, VAT, what your accountant says, regular bills, customers who pay late and things to watch |
| `people.md` | Your key customers and how formal to be with each, plus suppliers and advisers |
| `voice.md` | How you write and talk, so invoice notes and reminders sound like you |
| `tools.md` | Your computer and Claude setup, and the apps Claude is connected to |
