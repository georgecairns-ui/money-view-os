# Marketing notes: Money View OS

## In one sentence

Your money month by month, read from your accounts software and its bank feed, with a forecast of the next 12 weeks, invoices that go into your accounts software as drafts, and a tax pot the app works out for you to fill yourself. Claude never moves your money.

## Before and after, for the owner

**Before:** you know roughly what's in the bank, and you find out about a tight month when it arrives. Your invoices live in your accounts software, you chase late ones when you remember, the VAT bill and the tax bill are a surprise, and the money for tax is mixed up with the money you spend.

**After:** you open one page and see what's in the bank, who owes you, the tightest week in the next 12 and what to put aside for tax. You start an invoice in a minute and put it into Xero as a draft, or open Xero's new invoice page. Late ones come with a polite reminder, ready for you to send. When you try "can I afford a van?" you see what it does to your tightest week. Claude never moves money and never sends an invoice: you do, from your own accounts and bank.

## 3 things to show in a 60-second video

1. **The Overview.** Cash today, your lowest point, who owes you and your tax pot, and the 12 week chart. Rest the mouse on the tightest week and see what's in it.
2. **An invoice.** Write one in Money View, with the total and the VAT worked out, then press "Put it in Xero as a draft" (or "Open Xero to raise it" when the connection only reads). Say that you check it and send it yourself.
3. **A what if.** Ask "can I afford a van at £450 a month?" and watch the chart show the new line against "as things are", with the tightest week moving. Say it's an estimate.
